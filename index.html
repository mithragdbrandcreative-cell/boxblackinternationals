Frequently Asked Questions

01. How can I avoid hidden costs during construction?

We provide a fixed-cost BOQ (Bill of Quantities). Before construction begins, the materials, brands and engineering elements are documented and agreed upon. Unless you make changes to the design later, the agreed price remains the same through final handover.

02. How do you make sure my home is completed on time?

Box Black Internationals manages your villa construction in Chennai as a single, unified team, from architectural blueprints to final interior finishes. Our projects follow a milestone-driven schedule with planned procurement timelines to keep construction on track towards the agreed handover date.

03. What if I can't visit the construction site regularly?

You don't need to spend your weekends supervising your villa construction project. We provide weekly progress updates and high-resolution site photographs, keeping you informed about progress without having to visit the site regularly or manage contractors yourself.

04. How do you ensure transparency in materials and construction quality?

We ensure transparency through material verification, documentation and client approval. You can review and approve the material samples, while our Project Booklet and Handover Documentation record the structural and interior components used.

05. What happens if I find an issue after moving in?

BBI provides post-handover warranty support for your villa construction. If covered structural or finish-related issues arise within the applicable warranty period, our communication team coordinates the engineering response and required corrective work.

06.How do you ensure my villa design is executed as planned?

Box Black Internationals is an integrated engineering and construction company in Chennai, with architecture, floor planning, structural engineering and site execution managed under one roof. This helps ensure your villa design is carried through consistently from planning to construction.

Box Black Internationals delivers thoughtfully planned, expertly executed villa construction, combining design precision, quality craftsmanship, and transparent project management to bring your vision to life.
